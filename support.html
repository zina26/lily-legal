Lily — Manifest & Voice Reflect is a voice-first journaling app: you speak, and Lily reflects your own words back to you as your future self. Below are answers to the questions we hear most often. If yours isn't here, email us.

Subscription and billing

How do I cancel my subscription?

Your subscription is billed by Apple, so it is managed through your Apple account rather than by us. Either:

  • In Lily, open Settings → Account → Lily Pro → Manage subscription, which takes you to Apple's subscription page, or
  • On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions, choose Lily, and tap Cancel Subscription.

Cancelling stops the next renewal. You keep access until the end of the period you have already paid for.

I reinstalled Lily (or got a new phone) and it says I'm not subscribed.

Open Settings → Account → Lily Pro → Restore purchases. This asks Apple to re-attach your existing subscription to this device. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple account you used to subscribe.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are decided by Apple, not by us. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. If something in Lily went wrong that led you to ask, please also email us so we can fix it.

What happens to what I've already created if I cancel?

Everything Lily has already delivered to you — your reflections, affirmations, and songs — stays in the app and stays yours. A subscription is needed to generate new content, not to keep what you already have.

Your data and your account

How do I export my data?

Open Settings → Privacy & data → Export all data. Lily prepares a file with your entries, transcripts, and generated content, which you can save or share from the standard iOS share sheet.

How do I delete my account and all my data?

Open Settings → Privacy & data → Delete all data. This permanently deletes your recordings, transcripts, generated content, and your account from our servers. It cannot be undone. If you only want to remove individual Resets, you can do that from the Reset history without deleting your account.

Does deleting the app delete my data?

No. Deleting the app from your phone removes the local copy only. To delete what is stored on our servers, use Delete all data in Settings before you remove the app, or email us and we will delete it for you.

Voice and AI

What happens to my voice recordings?

When you speak to Lily, the recording is saved on your phone first. Where your iPhone supports it, the speech-to-text step runs on the device itself; otherwise it is done by a processing partner. Your recording and transcript are then stored in your account so Lily can reflect on them, and the text is sent to our AI partners to write Lily's response and to read it aloud. The full list of partners, what each one receives, and how long we keep things is in the Privacy Policy.

Is my content used to train AI?

No. Your content is used to generate your own reflections and is not used to train AI models. See the Privacy Policy for the specifics for each partner.

Lily's reply doesn't sound like it read what I said.

Occasionally a reply can be generic — for example when the connection dropped mid-way and Lily fell back to a safe default. Trying again usually fixes it. If it keeps happening, email us with roughly when it happened; that helps us find the cause.

Can I change Lily's voice?

Yes. Open Settings → Voice & listening → Lily Voice and preview the three voices before choosing. The change applies the next time Lily speaks.

A note on what Lily is

Lily is a reflection and journaling companion. It is not medical care, therapy, or a crisis service, and it should not be relied on for any of those.

If you are in crisis, thinking about harming yourself, or in immediate danger, please stop using Lily and contact a licensed professional, your local emergency number, or a crisis line in your country directly.

Signing in

I can't sign in / I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, use Forgot password to receive a reset link by email. If you originally signed up with Apple or Google, sign in with that same option. If you are still stuck, email us from the address you signed up with.
