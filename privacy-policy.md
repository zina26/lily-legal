# Lily Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

This policy explains what Lily ("we," "us," "our") collects when you use the app, how we use and share it, and the choices you have. Our contact details are in section 16.

## 1. Who can use Lily

Lily is intended for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18, and onboarding asks you to confirm your age before you can continue.

## 2. What we collect

### Information you provide

- **Your content:** voice recordings, transcripts, and text you enter, including journal entries and responses to reflection prompts. Recordings and text are saved on your device before being uploaded to private storage associated with your account.
- **Your preferences:** your preferred name, personal goals, identity words, preferred voice and tone, and reminder settings.
- **Account information:** your email address, if you choose to sign in with email, Apple, or Google.

### Content created for you

Lily creates personalized guidance, reflections, summaries, songs, and spoken audio based on what you share. These items, including summaries of your experiences and patterns over time, are personal information associated with your account.

### Information collected when you use Lily

We collect service activity, performance, and error information to keep Lily reliable. These records do not include your journal text, transcripts, lyrics, recordings, generated content, or account access credentials.

With your permission, Lily can access your microphone to record, save images to your photo library when you request it, and send reminders. You can manage these permissions in your device settings.

We do not track you across other apps or websites, use advertising identifiers, collect precise device location, or sell your personal information.

## 3. How we use what we collect

- To generate your morning guidance, evening follow-ups, Reset responses, songs, and spoken audio.

- To keep a continuous, private record of your journey so future content can draw on what you've actually said — never invented.

- To internally flag messages that appear to describe self-harm or a crisis, for safety monitoring and product improvement. This flag does not change whether or how Lily responds to your message.

- To maintain, secure, and improve the reliability of the service.

- To process your subscription and verify your access level.

- To respond if you contact us for support.

## 4. AI products and services

Lily uses artificial intelligence to support personal reflection and create personalized text, summaries, spoken audio, and songs.

### Our AI service providers

We use third-party AI services and technologies from **Anthropic, OpenAI, ElevenLabs, WaveSpeed, and Mureka**, including services delivered through **Amazon Web Services (AWS)** and WaveSpeed. These providers help us process language and audio and create content for you. We do not send every provider all of your information.

### How your information is used

Before sending your content to third-party AI services, we ask for your separate, explicit consent. Depending on the feature you use, the information processed may include your text, recordings, relevant preferences, summaries, and generated content. We send only the content needed for your request, not your full history.

Your recordings and text are saved on your device before upload. AI processing is subject to this policy and the applicable agreements and policies of the providers involved.

### AI training

We do not use your voice recordings to train AI models. We have not opted in to allow Anthropic to use your content for model training; Anthropic states that commercial API inputs and outputs are not used for training by default. This statement is specific to Anthropic and does not mean that all providers have identical data retention or training policies.

### Your choice

You can withdraw your consent to AI processing at any time in Settings. We will stop sending your content to AI providers for new processing. Content already generated remains available unless you delete it. Features that need new AI processing will no longer be available while consent is withdrawn.

AI-generated content may be inaccurate or miss important context. It is intended for personal reflection, not professional advice. You must not use Lily's AI features in ways that violate the applicable providers' terms.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect someone's safety, or in connection with a business transfer. If Lily is acquired or merged, this policy will continue to apply to your information under the new operator.

## 5. Voice and audio transcription

When you choose to speak instead of type, Lily saves your recording on your device and uploads it to private storage associated with your account. This storage is separate from transcription: a recording may still be uploaded even when transcription takes place on your device.

Where available, Lily first attempts to transcribe your recording on your device. If this is unavailable or unsuccessful, a third-party AI service described in section 4 processes the recording to produce a transcript.

We use your recordings to provide the content you request and keep the service reliable, not for marketing or AI model training. Recordings are shared for processing only with the AI service providers described in section 4, subject to the other disclosures in this policy. See section 9 for retention and deletion.

## 6. Your generated content stays private

The guidance, reflections, summaries, and songs Lily generates for you are visible only to you, inside your account. We do not publish, feature, or use them for marketing, and we do not let other users see them. If you delete a piece of generated content — or your whole account — it's removed the same way your own entries are (section 9).

## 7. Purchase and payment data

**We never see or store your payment card details.** Subscriptions are sold and billed entirely through Apple's App Store (or Google Play, for Android if offered). Apple and Google collect and hold your payment information directly — we only receive confirmation that a purchase happened and your subscription status.

Refunds are handled by Apple/Google according to their own policies, not by us directly.

## 8. Legal bases for processing (EU/UK)

If you are located in the EU or UK, this section applies to you. The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and UK GDPR require us to explain the legal basis we rely on for each way we process your personal information:

- **Performance of a contract** — providing the core service: storing your entries, showing your history, and processing your subscription.

- **Your consent** — sending your content to an AI provider to generate a response for you. This consent is asked for separately and explicitly (section 4), and you can withdraw it at any time.

- **Our legitimate interests** — diagnostics and reliability (keeping the service working), and responding when you contact us for support.

- **Vital interests** — internally flagging messages that appear to describe self-harm or a crisis, for safety monitoring and product improvement (section 3).

If any of your data is transferred outside your own region (for example, from the EU to the United States), we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or an equivalent safeguard recognized under GDPR/UK GDPR.

If you are located in the EEA or UK and you believe we are processing your personal information unlawfully, you have the right to complain to your national data protection authority.

## 9. How long we keep your data, and how to delete it

- **While your account is active:** we retain your entries, generated content, and memory summaries to maintain your personal history. They do not expire automatically. Service activity and diagnostic records are kept only as long as needed for reliability and troubleshooting.
- **Export your data:** you can export your entries, generated content, and memory summaries from Settings → Privacy & data.
- **Delete your account:** you can request deletion from the same screen. Account information and text content are removed from our active database immediately. Recordings, generated audio, and songs are deleted shortly afterward, normally within about a minute.
- **If deletion is interrupted:** the request remains pending and is retried automatically until it is complete.
- **Backups:** we do not currently maintain a separate long-term database backup. If this changes, we will update this policy with the backup retention period before the change takes effect.

## 10. Security

We use **Supabase** to host account information, entries, and generated content and to support sign-in and storage. It processes this information on our behalf under contractual restrictions, with retention as described in section 9.

We encrypt information sent between your device and our servers, restrict access to what is needed to operate the service, and use account-based access controls to protect your private content.

We take steps to protect your information, but no system or internet transmission is completely secure. We cannot guarantee that unauthorized access will never occur.

## 11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you

- **Correct** inaccurate data

- **Delete** your data

- **Export** your data in a portable format

- **Withdraw consent** to AI processing at any time

- **Object to or restrict** certain processing

- **Complain** to your local data protection authority

You can exercise the access, deletion, and export rights directly in the app (Settings → Privacy & data). For anything else, contact us at zinazx9726@gmail.com.

## 12. Mental health and safety

**Lily is not a medical service, a therapy service, or a crisis intervention service.** It does not diagnose, treat, or provide professional mental health advice, and it is not designed to recognize or respond to a mental health emergency.

**If you are in crisis, thinking about harming yourself, or in immediate danger, please stop using Lily and contact a licensed professional, your local emergency number, or a crisis line in your country directly.** Lily cannot help in a crisis and you should not rely on it for this.

For safety monitoring and product-improvement purposes, Lily may internally flag messages that appear to describe self-harm or a crisis. This flag is not shown to you, and it does not change whether or how Lily responds to your message.

## 13. Children's privacy

Lily is not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under that age. If we learn that we've collected data from someone under 18, we will delete it.

## 14. Do Not Track

Lily does not track you across other apps or websites. We do not currently change our practices in response to browser or device Do Not Track signals.

## 15. Changes to this policy

If we make a material change to this policy, we'll notify you in the app before the change takes effect. The date at the top of this page always reflects the most recent version.
