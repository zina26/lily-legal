This policy explains what Lily ("we," "us," "our") collects when you use the app, why, who it's shared with, and the choices you have. See section 16 for who operates Lily and how to reach us.

1. Who can use Lily

Lily is intended for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18, and onboarding asks you to confirm your age before you can continue.

2. What we collect

What you give us directly

  • Voice recordings, when you choose to speak instead of type. Recordings are saved on your device first, then uploaded to your private, account-scoped storage.
  • Transcribed text of what you said, once a recording has been processed.
  • Typed text, whenever you choose to type instead of speak (journal entries, Evening Reflection answers, Reset entries).
  • Onboarding preferences: your preferred name, the area of life you're focused on, words describing who you're becoming, your preferred tone of voice, and your reminder times.
  • Basic account details: your email address, if you choose to sign in with email, Apple, or Google.

What we generate for you

Lily uses your words to generate: your first welcome message, daily morning guidance, evening reflection follow-ups, Reset responses, personalized songs, the spoken-voice audio for any of the above, and periodic memory summaries (daily summaries, longer-running personal patterns, and weekly/cycle reflections). These generated items are personal data too — they are derived from what you said and tied to your account, even though you didn't type them yourself.

Information collected automatically

  • Diagnostic and operational records: a request ID, status, how long something took, a fixed failure code, which AI provider and model handled it, and cost accounting — used only to keep the service reliable. These records never contain your journal text, transcripts, lyrics, audio, generated content, or access tokens.
  • Device permissions, each requested only at the moment it's needed: microphone (when you choose to record instead of type), photo library add-only access (when you ask Lily to save a wallpaper or share-ready image), and notification permission (for your morning and evening reminders).

What we do not do: we do not track you across other apps or websites, we do not use advertising identifiers, and we do not use precise device location. We are not an advertising business, and we do not sell your data.

3. How we use what we collect

  • To generate your morning guidance, evening follow-ups, Reset responses, songs, and spoken audio.
  • To keep a continuous, private record of your journey so future content can draw on what you've actually said — never invented.
  • To internally flag messages that appear to describe self-harm or a crisis, for safety monitoring and product improvement. This flag does not change whether or how Lily responds to your message.
  • To maintain, secure, and improve the reliability of the service.
  • To process your subscription and verify your access level.
  • To respond if you contact us for support.

4. AI products and services

Lily is built around AI-generated content — that's the core of the product, not an add-on feature. Before Lily sends anything to an AI provider, we ask for your explicit, separate consent — this is not bundled into a general terms acceptance. On that screen we promise three things, and this policy confirms all three:

  • Saved on your device first — your recording or text is written to your device before anything is uploaded.
  • Only what's needed is sent — we send the minimum content required to produce that specific piece of content, not your full history.
  • Never used to train AI — Anthropic, whose model generates your written content, does not train on commercial API inputs or outputs by default, and we have not opted in to allow it. WaveSpeed, which handles audio (transcription, voice, and song generation), has separately confirmed to us directly that it does not use your data to train models.

You can withdraw this consent at any time in Settings. Once withdrawn, we stop sending your content to AI providers for new processing. Content already generated remains available to you unless you separately delete it.

We provide these AI features through two processors, each receiving only the minimum content needed for the one request it's handling, never your full history:

  • WaveSpeed: an aggregation platform we use for audio processing — transcription, voice generation, and song generation. It internally routes requests to specialized providers (including ElevenLabs for voice, and Mureka, Sonilo, and Google Lyria for music). WaveSpeed has confirmed directly to us (2026-09-07) that it does not use your data to train models. We keep our own copy of this data only as long as described in section 9; WaveSpeed's own published policy separately commits to a 7-day retention window for the output files it generates on our behalf.
  • OpenRouter, forwarding to Anthropic (Claude): generates the written content described above — morning guidance, evening reflection, Reset responses, memory summaries, and weekly/cycle reviews — from a text prompt built from your content. OpenRouter's own policy states it does not use your inputs or outputs to train models itself, and forwards them to the model provider you've configured (here, Anthropic) subject to that provider's terms. Anthropic's published commercial-API policy states: "By default, we will not use your inputs or outputs from our commercial products … to train our models." We use Anthropic's commercial API through OpenRouter, not their consumer Claude.ai product. OpenRouter's own policy also states it may transfer data to the US or countries outside the European Economic Area, and relies on EU standard contractual clauses and adequacy decisions for those transfers.

You must not use Lily's AI features in a way that violates WaveSpeed's or OpenRouter's/Anthropic's own terms of service.

AI-generated content is provided for reflection, not as professional advice. It can be wrong, generic, or miss context — it's produced by a language model reading your words, not a person who knows you.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect someone's safety (see section 12), or as part of a business transfer if Lily is ever acquired or merged — in which case this policy would continue to apply to your information under the new operator.

5. Voice and audio transcription

If you speak instead of type, your recording is saved to your device first, then uploaded to transcribe it into text. The audio and its transcript are processed by WaveSpeed (section 4) solely to produce that transcription or, for guidance and Reset audio, to produce spoken-voice output. We do not use your voice recordings for anything beyond generating the content you asked for and keeping the service reliable — not for marketing, not for training AI models, and not shared with anyone outside the processors described there.

6. Your generated content stays private

The guidance, reflections, summaries, and songs Lily generates for you are visible only to you, inside your account. We do not publish, feature, or use them for marketing, and we do not let other users see them. If you delete a piece of generated content — or your whole account — it's removed the same way your own entries are (section 9).

7. Purchase and payment data

We never see or store your payment card details. Subscriptions are sold and billed entirely through Apple's App Store (or Google Play, for Android if offered). Apple and Google collect and hold your payment information directly — we only receive confirmation that a purchase happened and your subscription status.

That confirmation is relayed through RevenueCat, which verifies and manages your subscription across devices. RevenueCat receives your purchase and subscription events, not your payment details — never your card number, billing address, or any other payment credential. If you sign in with Apple or Google, that same company also authenticates you using the identity token you choose to share, governed by its own privacy terms.

Refunds are handled by Apple/Google according to their own policies, not by us directly.

8. Legal bases for processing (EU/UK)

If you are located in the EU or UK, this section applies to you. The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and UK GDPR require us to explain the legal basis we rely on for each way we process your personal information:

  • Performance of a contract — providing the core service: storing your entries, showing your history, and processing your subscription.
  • Your consent — sending your content to an AI provider to generate a response for you. This consent is asked for separately and explicitly (section 4), and you can withdraw it at any time.
  • Our legitimate interests — diagnostics and reliability (keeping the service working), and responding when you contact us for support.
  • To be confirmed with legal — safety/crisis classification of your messages. This likely touches special-category health data and needs a stronger, more specific basis than general service provision; it is not yet assigned one of the bases above.

If you are located in the EEA or UK and you believe we are processing your personal information unlawfully, you have the right to complain to your national data protection authority.

9. How long we keep your data, and how to delete it

  • While your account is active, we keep your entries, generated content, and memory summaries for as long as your account exists — there is no automatic expiry, since the product is built on a continuous record of what you've shared. Diagnostic/ operational records (section 2) are kept only for as long as needed for reliability and debugging.
  • You can export all of your data at any time from Settings → Privacy & data. This includes your entries, generated content, and memory summaries.
  • You can delete your account and all associated data from the same screen. When you do: your account and content rows are removed from our database immediately, as part of that same request. Your stored media (recordings, generated audio, songs) is queued for deletion from storage and is normally cleared within about a minute by an automated process that runs continuously — not something that waits for a person to trigger it.
  • If a deletion request fails partway through, we do not mark it as complete — it stays in a pending state and is retried automatically, rather than silently telling you it succeeded when it hasn't.
  • We do not currently maintain a separate long-term backup copy of the database beyond the live system. If that changes, this section will be updated with the backup retention window before it takes effect.

10. Security

Your account data, content, and generated content are hosted on Supabase, our database, authentication, and storage infrastructure provider. Supabase acts strictly as our processor, under a contract that limits what it can do with your data, and retains it for as long as your account exists (section 9).

We use encryption in transit for all data sent between your device and our servers, and access to production data is limited to what's needed to operate the service. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee that unauthorized access will never happen — but our design choices (saving locally first, sending only what's needed, scoping every stored object to your account) are meant to limit what's exposed even if something does go wrong.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your data
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent to AI processing at any time
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Complain to your local data protection authority

You can exercise the access, deletion, and export rights directly in the app (Settings → Privacy & data). For anything else, contact us at zinazx9726@gmail.com.

12. Mental health and safety

Lily is not a medical service, a therapy service, or a crisis intervention service. It does not diagnose, treat, or provide professional mental health advice, and it is not designed to recognize or respond to a mental health emergency.

If you are in crisis, thinking about harming yourself, or in immediate danger, please stop using Lily and contact a licensed professional, your local emergency number, or a crisis line in your country directly. Lily cannot help in a crisis and you should not rely on it for this.

For safety monitoring and product-improvement purposes, Lily may internally flag messages that appear to describe self-harm or a crisis. This flag is not shown to you, and it does not change whether or how Lily responds to your message.

13. Children's privacy

Lily is not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under that age. If we learn that we've collected data from someone under 18, we will delete it.

14. Do Not Track

Most browsers and some mobile platforms offer a "Do Not Track" signal. There is no accepted industry standard yet for how apps should respond to it, so Lily does not currently respond to Do Not Track signals differently — this is largely moot for us in any case, since we don't track you across other apps or websites (section 2).

15. Changes to this policy

If we make a material change to this policy, we'll notify you in the app before the change takes effect. The date at the top of this page always reflects the most recent version.
